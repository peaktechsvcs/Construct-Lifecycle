---
name: Submittals model
description: Formal construction submittals are post-award packages; bid-stage substitutions and alternates remain linked source records.
---

Formal construction submittals belong to the awarded project or contract and are prepared before procurement, fabrication, or installation. Bid-stage substitutions, alternates, assumptions, and qualifications belong to the bid/proposal record and may seed a post-award submittal requirement without becoming the same record.

**Why:** AIA-style construction workflows treat submittals as technical compliance and coordination records after award, while procurement documents may require limited pre-bid substitution or early-procurement requests.

**How to apply:** Keep package, item, review disposition, and revision history tenant/environment-scoped under projects; preserve accepted bid links as origin metadata instead of merging bid and construction submission lifecycles.
