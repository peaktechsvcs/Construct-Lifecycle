# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and acquisition pages, currently `/` and `/pricing` (with `/subscribe` as an alias).
- Search, social-preview, and AI-crawler visibility of those public pages.
- Source-detectable WCAG 2.2 A/AA issues on public pages.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated workspace, administration, and internal operational routes.
- Transactional authentication, invitation, checkout-success, and checkout-cancellation pages except where their indexation affects public search quality.
- Legacy, design-system, mockup, and walkthrough artifacts that are not the primary deployed product.

## Target audience
- Construction suppliers and teams managing work from bid through closeout.

## Primary keywords
- Construction lifecycle platform
- Construction project lifecycle software
- Construction supplier project management

## Crawler assumptions
- Public acquisition pages should be indexable and shareable.
- Authenticated and transactional routes should not appear in search results.
- Social-preview and AI crawlers generally rely on the initial HTML response and do not execute the React application.
